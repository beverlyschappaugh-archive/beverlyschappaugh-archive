# Beverly Kay Dexter Schappaugh

<img src="beverly-schappaugh-memorial-portrait.jpg" alt="Beverly Kay Dexter Schappaugh memorial portrait born August 25 1941 Roaring Spring Pennsylvania elementary school teacher Valdosta Georgia murdered June 18 1990 Pine Grove Elementary School unsolved homicide" class="document-image">

<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>August 25, 1941 - June 18, 1990</strong> <em>Valdosta, Georgia</em></p>

Beverly Kay Dexter Schappaugh was born on August 25, 1941 in Roaring Spring, Pennsylvania, the daughter of John and Dorcus Dexter. She lived and worked in Valdosta, Georgia where she built a career as a dedicated elementary school educator. She earned a Master's degree and was among the early teachers who participated in the federal Head Start program in the late 1960s.

On June 18, 1990, Beverly Kay Schappaugh was found in the parking lot of Pine Grove Elementary School in Valdosta, Georgia. She was 48 years old. Her death was officially ruled a homicide. Her case remains unsolved.

This site exists to preserve a factual record of her life and death and to ensure that her story remains findable, documented, and not forgotten.
